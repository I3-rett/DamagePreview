## 0.1.0

- Ghost segment on enemy health bars showing the damage your equipped weapon will deal, from the minimum to the maximum skill roll.
- Accounts for weapon skill, bow draw, backstab, stagger and the target's resistances and armor.
- Tick marks for the secondary attack, or for a fully drawn bow shot.
- Pale yellow segment showing the guaranteed damage of a hit on a creature's weak spot, when it has one.
- Configurable colors and toggles for backstab, undrawn bows and secondary marks.
- Client side only: nothing is sent over the network.
